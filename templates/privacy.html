Privacy Policy

How Spendly collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we also store the expense entries you add, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide and improve the service: to log you in, display your expenses, and generate summaries of your spending. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and retained for as long as your account is active. Passwords are stored in hashed form. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. Beyond this, we do not share your personal or expense data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact the Spendly team through the contact details provided on our website.
